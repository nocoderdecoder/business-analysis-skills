# Review of the sample report

Five numerical claims were mapped. One matches, three differ from the supplied data, and one cannot be checked because November records are absent.

| Claim | Reported | Recalculated | Finding |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| October revenue | $36,000 | $36,000 | Match |
| Revenue growth | 25% | 20% | Mismatch |
| October conversion | 8.8% | 6.6667% | Mismatch |
| Conversion change | 1.5 percentage points | 0.6667 percentage points | Mismatch |
| November revenue | $40,000 | No source rows | Uncheckable |

October conversion is 400 orders divided by 6,000 visits. Taking the simple mean of the channel rates gives 8.8%, which assigns equal weight to channels with different visit counts.

The report's causal statement about the launch campaign is outside the calculator's scope. The sales export contains no evidence that isolates a campaign effect. The claim ledger therefore includes five calculator entries and one unresolved causal claim.

Source rows and exact decimal calculations are retained in `checks.json`. A matching calculation does not establish the completeness or accuracy of the source export.
